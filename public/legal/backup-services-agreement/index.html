    Available Storage Space. As part of the Services, you are given a pre-determined amount of storage space for 10Corp to perform backups of your Content.     Backup Duration. 10Corp will store the last 30 days of Content backups on a rolling basis and continue to store your Content backups for 30 days after the product has been cancelled. 

    Backup Management. As part of the Services, you will be able to manage your backups from your Account by viewing, downloading, restoring, and deleting your Content. You will also be able to select the frequency of your backups, including daily, every two days, weekly, or monthly. 10Corp will consistently back up your Content at your preferred frequency until your Services plan is cancelled or you run out of storage on your Services plan.
