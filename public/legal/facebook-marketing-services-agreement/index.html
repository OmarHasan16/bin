    Facebook Marketing Services Agreement

    It looks like you want a simplified explanation of the Facebook Marketing Services Agreement by 10Corp for customers who might not understand all the legal terms. Here’s a breakdown in plain English:

    Facebook Marketing Services Agreement – Simplified Guide

    1. What This Agreement Is About

    This agreement is a contract between 10Corp (the marketing agency) and the Client (the business owner or company hiring 10Corp). It defines the terms under which 10Corp will manage Facebook Ads for the Client.

    2. What 10Corp Will Do

    • 10Corp will run and manage Facebook Ads on behalf of the Client.
    • The Client allows 10Corp to create, change, and optimize ads to get better results.
    • The Client agrees on an ad budget before signing the contract.
    • The Client cannot make changes to the ad campaigns without 10Corp’s approval.
    • The service is billed monthly and applies to only one Facebook Ads account.

    3. What’s Included in the Service

    10Corp will:
    ✔ Increase visibility of the Client’s business on Facebook.
    ✔ Attract targeted customers to the Client’s website.
    ✔ Perform market research and audience targeting to find the best customers.
    ✔ Run A/B testing (comparing different ads to see what works best).
    ✔ Set up tracking tools to measure performance.
    ✔ Provide weekly and monthly reports with results and suggestions.

    4. How 10Corp Handles Facebook Ads

    • The Client gives 10Corp access to their Facebook Ads & Business account.
    • 10Corp will handle ad creation, audience targeting, and retargeting (showing ads to people who have already visited the website).
    • 10Corp charges management fees separate from Facebook’s ad costs.
    • If the Client cancels the service, Facebook may still charge for running ads.

    5. Payments & Fees

    • The Client must pay an initial setup fee and a monthly management fee for the service.
    • Invoices are sent at the start of each month and must be paid by the 20th.
    • The agreement includes cancellation rules and details on refunds.

    6. Who Owns the Work? (Intellectual Property)

    • 10Corp owns the ad designs and content it creates unless otherwise agreed.
    • 10Corp may use the created materials for promotion unless the Client objects.

    7. What 10Corp Guarantees

    • 10Corp promises to provide professional services with care.
    • Ad performance is not guaranteed, but best practices will be used.
    • 10Corp may hire subcontractors (other experts) to assist.
    • Some changes may be necessary to follow Facebook’s policies or the law.

    8. When 10Corp Is Not Responsible

    If there are uncontrollable events (e.g., natural disasters, Facebook policy changes, technical issues), 10Corp is not responsible for delays.

    9. What the Client Promises

    • The Client must legally own or have permission to use any content (e.g., images, videos, trademarks).
    • The Client is responsible for any legal issues that arise from using unauthorized content.

    10. Keeping Information Private

    • Both 10Corp and the Client agree to keep all advertising and business details confidential.

    11. Ending the Agreement

    • If the Client wants to cancel, they must follow the notice period and payment terms.
    • The Client must pay for any ads or services already purchased.

    12. Entire Agreement

    • This agreement replaces all previous discussions between 10Corp and the Client.

    13. What Happens if Part of the Agreement is Invalid?

    • If any section of this agreement is not legally valid, the rest of the agreement still applies.

    14. How Communication Works

    • All official notices must be sent by email, unless both parties agree to another method.

    15. How Disputes Are Handled

    • If there is a disagreement, both parties should try to resolve it through mediation before taking legal action.

    16. Legal Matters

    • This agreement follows New Maxico City law.
    • Any legal disputes must be handled in New Maxico City courts.

    17. Final Acceptance

    • Both the Client and 10Corp must sign the agreement before services begin.
