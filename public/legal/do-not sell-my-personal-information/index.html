    Do Not Sell My Personal Information

    California Privacy Notice

    This Privacy Notice Addendum for California Residents (“CA Privacy Notice”) supplements the Web Services LLC, a New Mexico limited liability company ("10Corp") Privacy Notice** and applies solely to residents of California, including job applicants. If you receive a job offer, additional privacy notices and requests for information may apply.

    This CA Privacy Notice does not apply when we act as a service provider processing personal information on behalf of our customers.

    Your Rights Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

    If the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) or other data protection laws apply, you have the right to:

    • Request information about the personal data we collect, including sources, business purposes, and third-party sharing.
    • Opt-out of sharing your personal information with third parties.
    • Request deletion of your personal data in certain circumstances.

    To learn more about the categories of personal information we collect and how we use it, refer to the sections “Categories of Information We Collect From You” and “How We Use Your Information” in our Privacy Notice.

    Personal Information We Collect

    We collect personal information that identifies, relates to, or could be linked to you. However, the following do not qualify as personal information under the CCPA:

    • Publicly available information from government records.
    • Deidentified, aggregated, or anonymized information that cannot be linked back to you.

    Categories of Personal Information Collected

    We collect personal information from various sources, such as when you attend events, visit our website, or apply for a job.

    Category Examples
    Personal Identifiers Name, alias, date of birth, driver’s license, passport number.
    Contact Information Home address, phone number, email.
    Commercial Information Services purchased, usage data.
    Internet/Network Data IP address, browser, device information.
    Other Personal Information Social media interactions, event participation.
    Inferences Predictions about interests and preferences.
    Sensitive Personal Information Social Security number, driver’s license (for identity verification).

    Information Collected from Job Applicants

    If you apply for a job, we may collect additional information:

    Category Examples
    Pre-Hire Information Resume, interview notes, reference checks.
    Protected Classifications Race, gender, age, disability (if voluntarily disclosed).
    Employment History Prior experience, job positions, salary history (if permitted by law).
    Education Information Degrees, certifications, transcripts.
    Inferences Skills, aptitude, behavioral analysis for hiring decisions.

    How We Use Your Personal Information

    We use collected information for various business purposes, such as:

    • Hiring & Employment – Evaluating job applications, managing payroll, and verifying employment references.
    • Legal Compliance – Meeting regulatory requirements and maintaining company records.
    • Business Operations – Managing accounts, verifying identities, and improving services.
    • Marketing & Advertising – Delivering personalized ads and promotions (with consent).
    • Security & Fraud Prevention – Detecting suspicious activity and ensuring compliance.

    Disclosure of Personal Information

    We do not sell your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. However, we may share information with trusted service providers, vendors, and partners for business purposes.

    Third-Party Disclosures

    Category Shared With
    Personal Identifiers Service providers, partners.
    Commercial Information Business partners, vendors.
    Internet/Network Data Security providers, analytics platforms.
    Other Personal Information Social media partners, third-party service providers.

    Opting Out of the Sale of Personal Information

    While we do not sell your personal data in the traditional sense, we may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience. Under CCPA, this could be considered a “sale.”

    To opt-out of such data sharing:

    • Update your Cookie Settings by clicking the link in the footer of our website.
    • Submit a request through our portal or by contacting us.

    Exercising Your California Privacy Rights

    As a California resident, you can request:

    • The Right to Know – Information about the personal data we collect and share.
    • The Right to Request Deletion – Removal of personal data, subject to legal limitations.
    • The Right to Opt-Out – Stop the sale or sharing of your personal data.
    • The Right to Non-Discrimination – No penalties for exercising your privacy rights.

    To exercise these rights, you may:

    • Submit a request via our portal.
    • Log into your online account and adjust privacy settings.
    • Visit our Privacy Center.
    • Contact us at privacy@10corp.com.

    We will verify your identity before processing any request. If using an authorized agent, you will need to provide written confirmation.

    10Corp as a Service Provider

    When we act as a service provider to other companies, we process personal data on their behalf. If you are an end-user of one of our customers, you should contact them directly to exercise your rights.

    Need More Information?

    For additional details or to request a copy of this CA Privacy Notice, visit our website or email us at privacy@10corp.com.
