## **Certificate Descriptions**

The SSL Certificates available through Spaceship include the following:

- **InstantSSL, EV SSL, EV Multi-domain, Unified Communications, and PremiumSSL Wildcard Certificates**: These are fully authenticated certificates offering domain authentication, message integrity, and encryption. They assure the identity of the Subscriber organization and its entitlement to use the domain name listed.
- **PositiveSSL, PositiveSSL Wildcard, and PositiveSSL Multi-domain Certificates**: These certificates are domain-validated and provide assurances of domain validity and encryption. However, they do not involve organization authentication.

## **Use Restrictions**

You are prohibited from using your Certificate for the following purposes:
- **Third-Party Usage**: Using your Certificate for or on behalf of another organization.
- **Unauthorized Domain/Organization**: Performing operations in connection with any domain or organization name other than the one submitted in your Certificate Application.
- **Hazardous Operations**: Using the Certificate in systems where failure could lead to severe harm, such as in nuclear facilities, aircraft systems, or weapons control.
