This Global Privacy Notice applies to all individuals regardless of where they live. We also maintain customized local privacy notices for certain jurisdictions with unique privacy notice requirements.

**Data Covered by this Privacy Notice**

This global privacy notice covers personal data when we act as the data controller.

* **Personal data** includes data that identifies or could be used to identify a specific person (e.g., name, address, payment details).
* We act as a **controller** when we process personal data for our own use (e.g., when you open an account).

This notice does not apply when our customers process personal data for their own benefit (e.g., when you send emails using our hosting service). In those cases, you are the "controller" and we are the "processor".

### Core Privacy Rights

We recognize several core privacy rights for all individuals:

1.  The right to know what personal data we hold about you.
2.  The right to access, correct, or delete your personal data.
3.  The right to transfer your personal data (data portability).
4.  The right to set your individual marketing and advertising preferences.

If we do not honor a request for legal or other reasons, we will explain why we did not honor the request.

---

### PII We Collect

1.  **Personal Data Individuals Provide**
    We collect personal data when a customer sets up an account, uses our services, or contacts us. Examples: Name, email address, phone number, physical address, and payment method.

2.  **Personal Data We Collect Automatically**
    We collect data automatically when you visit our websites. Examples: Device ID, IP address, browser type, and page views.

3.  **Personal Data from Other Sources**
    We may collect data from publicly available sources, social media, or third-party data providers.

4.  **Personal Data We Generate**
    We process data to generate inferences and insights, such as using data analytics to improve our services.

* Marketing and advertising our services.

### Disclosures to Others

We may disclose personal data to:
* **Processors:** To provide services like payment processing, security, and customer support.
* **Business Partners:** To offer services like SSL certificates or domain registration.
* **Legal Compliance:** To comply with law enforcement or protect our legal rights.
* **Business Transfers:** If we sell assets or merge with another company.

---

### Cookies & Identifiers

We use cookies, web beacons, and scripts to provide customized services, measure performance, and prevent fraud.

* **Cookies:** Text files placed on your device to remember preferences.
* **Web Beacons:** Image files used to track email opens or ad views.
* **Scripts:** Code that powers interactive features.

You can manage optional identifiers through your browser settings. However, mandatory identifiers (required for security and service operation) cannot be disabled.

---

### International Transfers

We transfer personal data internationally to operate our business. We comply with applicable laws when making these transfers.

* **EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework:** We adhere to standards for transferring data from the EU/EEA, Switzerland, and the UK.
* If you are located outside the United States, please note that we transfer data to the United States for processing.

### Security & Retention

* **Security:** We use risk-based measures (technical and physical) to protect personal data. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we strive to protect your information.
* **Retention:** We retain data only as long as necessary for business needs or legal compliance. When no longer needed, data is deleted or de-identified.
