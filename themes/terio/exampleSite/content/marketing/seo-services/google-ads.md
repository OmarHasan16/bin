## **Google Ads FAQ**  

### **How does Google Ads work?**  
Google Ads operates on a **bidding system**, where advertisers bid on keywords. When users search for those keywords, Google may display the ad, and advertisers only pay if the ad is clicked.  

### **How much does it cost to advertise on Google Ads?**  
The cost depends on the competition of your keywords and industry. On average, small businesses spend **$1–$10 per click**.  

### **What should my Google Ads budget be?**  
Your budget depends on your **business goals, competition, and target audience**. A good starting point is **$300–$1000/month** for small businesses.  

### **Is Google Ads free?**  
No, Google Ads is a paid advertising platform. However, **Google does offer promotional credits** for new advertisers.  

### **Is it worth paying for Google Ads?**  
Yes! Google Ads provides measurable results, advanced targeting, and **high ROI** when managed properly.  

### **How can I track my campaign performance?**  
You can track campaign performance using Google Ads' built-in analytics, which provides insights on impressions, clicks, conversions, and ROI.
