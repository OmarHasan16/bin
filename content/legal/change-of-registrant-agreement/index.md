# 10Corp Change of Registrant Agreement  
**Last Revised: December 10, 2023**  

## 1. Overview
This Domain Name Change of Registrant Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between 10Corp and you upon electronic acceptance. It governs the transfer of a registered domain name from a current registrant (“Prior Registrant”) to a new registrant (“New Registrant”).

By accepting this Agreement, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by:
- This Agreement
- Our [Universal Terms of Service](#)
- The Domain Name Registration and Transfer Agreements
- The [Uniform Domain Name Dispute Resolution Policy](#)

- **“We”, “us”, “our”** refer to 10Corp.
- **“You”, “your”, “User”, “Customer”** refer to the individual or entity accepting this Agreement.
- No third-party rights are conferred under this Agreement.

## 3. Modifications to the Agreement
We reserve the right to update this Agreement and related policies at any time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting. Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of revisions.

### Notification of Changes
We may notify users via email about updates. Keeping your account information up to date is your responsibility. We are not liable for undelivered notifications due to outdated contact details.

## 4. Change of Registrant and Transfer Process
### A. Change of Registrant
- Both the Prior and New Registrants must consent to the transfer.
- Confirmation emails will be sent to both parties for approval.
- A five (5) day window is provided for approval, failing which the request expires.
- A sixty (60) day transfer lock applies after a registrant change unless explicitly opted out.

### B. Transfer Process
- The Prior Registrant initiates a transfer request.
- The New Registrant must confirm acceptance within ten (10) days.
- Failure to confirm will result in the expiration of the transfer request.

## 5. Legal Responsibilities
### Representations and Warranties
- The Prior and New Registrants confirm their legal right to make the transfer.
- The New Registrant assumes full responsibility for the domain name upon transfer.

### Liability Limitations
- 10Corp is not liable for disputes over domain ownership or invalid transfers.
- We are not responsible for service interruptions or data loss during the transfer process.

## 6. Additional Terms for .US Registrations
Registrants of .US domains must:
- Be U.S. citizens, entities, or organizations with a presence in the U.S.
- Maintain accurate and verifiable contact information.

Failure to meet these criteria may result in immediate cancellation of the domain registration.
