## **2. Identity Verification**  

To process your dispute, provide proof of identity.

### **A. Individual Registrants**  
📎 Attach a copy of a **government-issued photo ID** (e.g., driver’s license or passport).  
🔹 *Ensure the ID is clearly visible. If faxing, lighten the photo ID for clarity.*

### **B. Business Registrants**  
If a company was listed as the registrant, provide:

1️⃣ **Government-issued photo ID** of the business representative signing below.  
2️⃣ **One of the following company verification documents:**  
   - Business license  
   - Tax certificate (*Tax number alone is not acceptable*)  
   - "Doing Business As" (DBA) documentation  
   - Fictitious Name Registration  
   - IRS 501(c)(3) Determination Letter (for nonprofits)  
   - State-issued tax exemption certificate (for charities)
