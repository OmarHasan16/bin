# Addendum for California Users

**Effective Date: December 10, 2023**

This Privacy Notice Addendum for California Residents (the “CA Privacy Notice”) supplements the information in the [Web Services LLC. Privacy Notice](https://www.10corp.com/privacy-center) and applies solely to California residents, including job applicants (“consumers” or “you”).

**Note:** This CA Privacy Notice does not apply when we act as a service provider processing personal information on behalf of customers.

## Your Rights Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

Where applicable under the CCPA, you may:

- **Request access** to categories of personal information we collect, its sources, business purposes, third-party sharing, and specific details we hold about you.
- **Opt out** of personal information disclosure to third parties.
- **Request deletion** of your personal information in specific circumstances.

## Information We Collect

We collect personal information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with a consumer or device. Exclusions include:

- Publicly available information from government records.
- Deidentified, aggregated, or anonymized data.

For detailed categories of collected data, refer to [our Privacy Notice](https://www.10corp.com/privacy-center).

### Categories of Personal Information Collected

We collect information from various sources, including website interactions, job applications, and business engagements. This includes:

- **Personal Identifiers:** Name, date of birth, driver’s license, passport number.
- **Contact Information:** Address, phone number, email.
- **Commercial Information:** Services purchased, usage details.
- **Internet/Network Data:** IP address, browser/device details.
- **Sensitive Personal Information:** Social Security number, identification for verification.

### Job Applicant Data

For employment considerations, we collect:

- **Pre-Hire Information:** Application details, background checks, interview notes.
- **Protected Classifications:** Race, gender, age (as required by law).
- **Employment & Education History:** Work experience, degrees, certifications.

## How We Use Personal Information

We use personal data for legitimate business purposes, including:

- Fulfilling service requests and employment processing.
- Ensuring compliance with legal and regulatory obligations.
- Enhancing security, website functionality, and personalized experiences.

For a full list, see the [Privacy Notice](https://www.10corp.com/privacy-center).

## Sharing and Disclosure of Personal Information

### Categories of Third-Party Disclosures

In the last 12 months, we have shared:

- **Identifiers & Contact Information:** Service providers, partners.
- **Commercial & Network Information:** Vendors, corporate affiliates.

### Sale of Personal Information

We may use third-party tracking technologies (e.g., cookies, web beacons) that could be considered a “sale” under CCPA.

- **Categories of Sold Information:** Identifiers, internet activity.
- **Opt-Out:** Manage preferences via the [Cookie Settings](https://www.10corp.com/privacy-center/cookie-policy).

### Your California Privacy Rights

#### **Right to Know**

You can request details on:

- Specific personal data we hold.
- Categories of collected, shared, or sold information.
- Business or commercial purposes for data collection and sharing.

#### **Right to Deletion**

You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal exceptions.

#### **Right to Opt-Out**

To stop the sale of personal information, update your [Cookie Settings](https://www.10corp.com/privacy-center/cookie-policy).

#### **Right to Non-Discrimination**

We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

## How to Exercise Your Rights

To make a request:

- Use our [DSAR Portal](https://www.10corp.com/privacy-center/requests).
- Log into your account settings.
- Visit our [Privacy Center](https://www.10corp.com/privacy-center).
- Call our toll-free number (found on your service homepage).
- Email: [privacy@10corp.com](mailto:privacy@10corp.com).

Verification may be required before processing requests.

## Retention of Personal Information

We retain personal data per our data retention policy. For details, email [privacy@10corp.com](mailto:privacy@10corp.com).
