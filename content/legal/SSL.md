## **2. Certificate Descriptions**

Spaceship offers the following SSL Certificates:

- **InstantSSL, EV SSL, EV Multi-domain, Unified Communications, and PremiumSSL Wildcard Certificates**: Fully authenticated certificates providing domain authentication, message integrity, and encryption. These ensure the identity of the Subscriber organization and its entitlement to use the domain name listed.
- **PositiveSSL, PositiveSSL Wildcard, and PositiveSSL Multi-domain Certificates**: Domain-validated certificates ensuring domain ownership and encryption but do not authenticate the organization.
