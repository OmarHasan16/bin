## Domain Transfer FAQs

### How do I transfer a domain from one host to another?
First, check if your domain meets transfer requirements, obtain your authorization code, and submit your domain for transfer. Learn more in our [How to transfer a domain](/help/domain-transfer) article.

### Why do I have to wait 60 days to transfer my domain?
ICANN requires a 60-day lock after registration or transfer to prevent hijacking. Read more in our [Canceled transfers](/help/canceled-transfers) article.

### How do I transfer a domain name to another person?
If you want to move your domain to another 10Corp account, use the **Change Ownership** option. Note that ownership transfers are final.

### How do I check the transfer status of my domain?
Log into your 10Corp account and follow the steps in our [Transfer statuses](/help/transfer-status) article.

### How long does a domain transfer take?
Transfers typically take **30 minutes to 6 days**, depending on the registrar. Read our [How long does a domain transfer take?](/help/transfer-time) guide.

### What are some domain transfer limitations?
Transfers may be denied due to fraud, ownership disputes, or if requirements aren’t met.

### How can I transfer a domain name to another registrar?
Ensure your domain is **60+ days old**, hasn’t been transferred in 60 days, has Whois Privacy disabled, and is unlocked. Then, initiate the transfer. [More](/help/transfer-process)

### Can I transfer a domain name within 10Corp?
Yes, you can instantly transfer a domain between 10Corp accounts. [More](/help/internal-transfers)

### Can I transfer my website, email, and other services?
Yes! Our team can assist you in moving your hosting, email, and other services. Contact [Support](/support) for help.

### What does it cost to transfer a domain?
Pricing varies by extension but is typically **less than renewal costs**. A transfer includes **1-year registration extension and free Advanced Security**. [More](/help/transfer-cost)

### How long does a domain transfer take to or from 10Corp?
Transfers **to** 10Corp depend on the losing registrar but usually take **5-7 days**. Transfers **away** from 10Corp take the same time but can be expedited by contacting the losing registrar. [More](/help/transfer-duration)
